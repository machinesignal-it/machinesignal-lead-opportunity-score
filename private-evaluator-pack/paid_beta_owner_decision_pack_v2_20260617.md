# MachineSignal - Paid Beta Owner Decision Pack v2

Date: 2026-06-17  
Status: owner decision pack, not an activation  
Audience: owner, agents, fiscal/admin readiness, legal/privacy readiness

## Executive Decision

MachineSignal is technically ready for the current sandbox scope.

MachineSignal is not ready to take real money, issue invoices, collect payment methods, process real customer datasets, process personal data, issue production API keys, perform external outreach, publish to paid marketplaces or launch hosted public MCP.

The correct current decision is:

> Prepare the paid beta, but keep activation blocked.

## What This Pack Is

This pack is the current operating decision document for moving from sandbox testing toward a controlled paid beta.

It does not approve paid beta.

It does not approve commercial go-live.

It does not approve real payments, invoices, payment method collection, real data, personal data, external outreach, public marketplace publication, hosted public MCP or MCP registry submission.

## Current Evidence

Latest validated evidence:

- technical sandbox complete for the current scope;
- advisor gate complete for the current scope;
- Company Brain aligned with current status;
- OpenAPI, onboarding and catalog explicitly state sandbox/no-go commercial status;
- internal docs/API examples refinement probe: 62/62 checks passed;
- Company Brain alignment probe: 44/44 checks passed;
- advisor gate rehearsal: 18 tests, 0 hard-block violations, 0 unexpected allows.

## Decision Options

### Option A - Continue Sandbox Only

Meaning:

- keep all work in sandbox;
- no paid beta preparation beyond documentation;
- no real customer involvement;
- no fiscal/payment setup yet.

Recommended when:

- the owner wants more technical confidence;
- the owner does not want fiscal/legal/payment decisions yet.

Effect:

- lowest risk;
- no revenue test;
- slower validation of commercial demand.

### Option B - Prepare Controlled Paid Beta, Do Not Activate

Meaning:

- prepare all owner decision materials;
- prepare policy drafts;
- prepare fiscal/admin questions;
- prepare legal/privacy questions;
- prepare payment/test-mode architecture;
- keep all commercial gates blocked.

Recommended now.

Effect:

- moves toward monetization without taking money;
- keeps legal/fiscal/payment risk low;
- gives the owner a concrete checklist for later approval.

### Option C - Activate Controlled Paid Beta

Meaning:

- allow limited real beta customers;
- allow approved production keys;
- allow real payments or invoices;
- allow customer support obligations.

Not recommended today.

Reason:

- fiscal/admin path is not approved;
- legal/privacy path is not approved;
- payment method and invoice process are not approved;
- real data policy is not approved;
- production key policy is not owner-approved;
- support and refund responsibility are not final.

## Minimum Conditions Before Paid Beta Activation

Paid beta can be considered only after all of these are true:

1. Fiscal/admin readiness is approved.
2. Legal/privacy readiness is approved.
3. Payment and invoice path is approved.
4. Product catalog and listino are approved.
5. Credit and replacement rules are approved.
6. Refund policy is approved.
7. Production API key policy is approved.
8. Real-data and personal-data policy is approved.
9. Support and post-sale policy is approved.
10. Cost cap and kill switch are approved.
11. Distribution channel is approved.
12. Owner signs a final go/no-go page.

## Required Owner Inputs

The owner must decide:

- whether to stay sandbox-only or prepare paid beta;
- whether to open/prepare the fiscal setup before any money is accepted;
- which payment path is preferred: manual invoice, prepaid credits, checkout, or subscription;
- whether real customer lists are allowed later, and under what rules;
- whether beta customers are private/manual approval only;
- whether external distribution is delayed until after fiscal/legal/payment readiness.

## Recommended Operating Route

Recommended route:

1. Keep current system sandbox-only.
2. Prepare advisor question pack for fiscal/admin and legal/privacy.
3. Prepare final paid beta approval checklist.
4. Prepare P&L delta for paid beta only.
5. Prepare production-key and support policy drafts.
6. Ask owner to decide whether to move to controlled paid beta.
