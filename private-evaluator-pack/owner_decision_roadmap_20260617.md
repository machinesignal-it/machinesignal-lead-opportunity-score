# MachineSignal - Owner Decision Roadmap

Date: 2026-06-17
Status: decision roadmap, no commercial activation

## Simple Status

MachineSignal has completed the current technical sandbox test scope.

This does not mean the business is live.

It means the technical product can now be prepared for a controlled paid beta, while payments, invoices, production keys, real data, outreach and public marketplace/MCP publication remain blocked.

## Track 2 - Decisions To Validate Externally

These should not be guessed by agents.

They should be validated with an accountant, fiscal advisor, legal/privacy advisor or equivalent professional.

| Topic | Question To Validate | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Fiscal setup | Can we accept beta payments before P.IVA/company setup? | Avoid tax/accounting problems |
| Invoicing | How would invoices or receipts be issued? | Required before real money |
| VAT/P.IVA | Is P.IVA required before paid beta? | Core commercial blocker |
| Legal terms | Are beta terms sufficient? | Limits misunderstanding and liability |
| Privacy/data | Can we process customer lists or domains? | Controls privacy/data risk |
| DPA/data retention | Do we need a data processing agreement? | Important if customers submit data |
| Refund policy | Are credit replacements enough or are cash refunds needed? | Commercial and legal clarity |
| Liability | How do we state that scores are decision support, not guaranteed outcomes? | Avoid overpromising |

Recommended external validation: fiscal/P.IVA and privacy/data first.
